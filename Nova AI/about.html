About QuBix AI

Welcome to QuBix AI — your intelligent AI assistant.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make useful AI tools accessible through a simple, fast and friendly experience, on any device.

What QuBix AI Offers

  • Smart AI engine — choose a model yourself or let QuBix pick one automatically. If one model is busy or unavailable, QuBix switches to another.
  • Chat history — saved conversations you can search, pin, export and share with a read-only link.
  • Web research — optional web search with numbered sources and links, plus a research mode for deeper answers.
  • Memory — QuBix can remember your preferences and useful facts to personalize answers. You control it: add, delete or clear memories, or switch automatic remembering off.
  • File and image understanding — attach PDF, DOCX, text and code files, read scanned PDFs, and ask questions about images.
  • Image generation — create images from text descriptions, saved with your chat.
  • Projects — group chats into projects, each with its own instructions.
  • AI Tools — Summarizer, Translator, Writer, Code Helper and Email Writer.
  • Prompt library — save and reuse your favorite prompts, with fill-in placeholders.
  • Chat summaries — summarize a conversation and continue with suggested follow-up questions.
  • Usage dashboard — see your requests, models used and response times.
  • Installable app — add QuBix AI to your phone or desktop home screen.

A Note on AI

AI can make mistakes. Please double-check important information, code and translations before relying on them.

Built to Grow

QuBix AI is continuously evolving, with the goal of adding more useful features and improving the experience.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to QuBix AI. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used and what choices you have when you use our service.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account details used to sign you in.
  • Chats and messages you send and receive, including text extracted from files you attach.
  • Generated images created in your chats, which are stored so they remain available when you return.
  • Memories you add yourself, or that QuBix saves automatically from facts you mention in your messages (for example a preference or a project).
  • Preferences and content you create, such as language and response style, projects and project instructions, and saved prompts.
  • Usage records: which feature you used, which AI provider and model answered, response time, whether the request succeeded or failed, error messages and character counts, with timestamps. These records do not contain the text of your messages.
  • Browser storage on your device, such as your sign-in session, selected model, selected project, theme, automatic-memory setting and cached chat summaries.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide AI chat, tools, web research, image generation and file understanding.
  • To save and display your chat history, projects, prompts and memories.
  • To personalize answers using your preferences, project instructions and relevant memories.
  • To show you your own usage dashboard and to keep the service reliable and secure.

4. Files and Images You Attach

PDF, DOCX, text and code files are read in your browser, and the extracted text is sent with your message to the AI. Scanned PDFs and images you attach are sent to Google Gemini for text recognition or image analysis. QuBix AI does not store these attached files themselves; only the resulting conversation text is saved.

5. Third-Party Services

QuBix AI relies on the following services. Information sent to them is subject to their own privacy policies.

  • Supabase — sign-in, database and image storage.
  • Netlify — hosting and the server functions that process requests.
  • Groq and Google Gemini — AI responses, summaries, tools, memory extraction, image understanding and text recognition.
  • Tavily — web search. When web search is on, your question is sent to Tavily.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI — image generation. Your image description (translated to English by AI when needed) is sent to Cloudflare.

6. Shared Conversations and Images

If you create a share link, anyone who has the link can view that conversation, including its generated images, without signing in. Generated images are stored at private-looking, hard-to-guess web addresses, but anyone who has an image's address can open it. You can disable all share links for a chat at any time using “Stop sharing” in the share window. Deleting a chat also removes its share links, and we try to delete its stored images.

7. Memory and Personalization

Automatic memory can be turned off in the Memories window. We try not to save sensitive details such as passwords, payment or ID numbers, but this cannot be guaranteed, so please avoid sharing them in chat. You can delete individual memories or clear all of them at any time.

8. Data Security

We use available security measures, including access rules that restrict your stored data to your own account. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it or ask us to. You can delete chats (with their messages and generated images), memories, projects and prompts yourself in the app. To delete your account and the data linked to it, including usage records, contact the service operator.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the QuBix AI service operator.

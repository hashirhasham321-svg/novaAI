Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QuBix AI handles information when you use our website and AI services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use QuBix AI, information may include:

  • Account information, such as your email address.
  • Messages and conversation history you choose to save.
  • Preferences, custom instructions and saved memories.
  • Information you submit when using features such as feedback or chat sharing.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide AI responses, save and display conversations, maintain account sessions, remember preferences, provide sharing features, and improve the reliability of the service.

3. AI and Third-Party Services

QuBix AI may use third-party services to operate its features. These may include Supabase for authentication and database services, Groq and Google Gemini for AI responses, and Tavily for web search.

When you use these features, relevant information such as your prompts may be sent to the service responsible for processing that request. Those providers process information under their own policies and terms.

4. Chat Sharing

If you create a public share link, people who have that link may be able to view the shared conversation. Do not share private or sensitive information in a conversation you intend to make public.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

The website or its service providers may use browser storage or similar technologies to maintain settings, preferences, authentication sessions, and website functionality.

6. Data Security

We use services and technical measures intended to protect information. However, no website, online service, or method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You should use the available account and application controls to manage conversations, preferences, and saved memories. You may also stop using the service. Deleting information from the application may not automatically remove copies held by third-party providers or required for legitimate technical or legal purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

QuBix AI is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, please use the contact method provided on the QuBix AI website, if available.
